WA211 acknowledged the findings, with one team member confirming the search-discoverability fix as a known issue.

04 Reflection

Lessons learned from running sessions with participants who have Traumatic Brain Injury.

Scheduling
Issues

Pts would often “forget” about session

Solution
  1. Source better scheduling software
  2. Include more reminders/notifications
  3. Have a reminder email + joining instructions earlier
Troubleshooting
Issues

Some pts would encounter challenging troubleshooting issues

Solution
  1. Develop better troubleshooting instructions
  2. Prepare for multiple devices
Screening Criteria
Issues

Pts used multiple devices we weren’t prepared to collect data or troubleshoot with.

Solution
  1. Incorporate device question
  2. Refine tech literacy questions
